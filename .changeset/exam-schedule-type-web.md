A schedule now has a Jenis ujian (Ulangan Harian, Quiz, Tugas, UTS or UAS), chosen in Tambah Jadwal and Ubah Jadwal and locked once students have started. The schedule list and the Penilaian Ujian list show it in a Jenis column. It needs exam-service with the exam type, which rejects a schedule without one.

The Sesi dialog and the session list of Penilaian Ujian gain a Keluar column with how many times the app reported the student leaving the exam; an exam the app handed in after more than three exits shows 4× or more.
