Add the exam grading and exam score screens. Teachers open Penilaian Ujian to see the exams of their classes with how many students wait for a grade, open an exam to list its students, and grade each short answer and essay (score up to the question points, optional note) next to the question, its grading guide and the student's answer. The last grade closes the session and its score is sent to the assessment. Nilai Ujian lists the exam scores of the classes the teacher teaches. Schedules with sessions lose Batalkan and gain Sesi, a dialog that lists each student with status, score and device and lets staff with exam-sessions.release free the device of a session in progress. Routes /penilaian-ujian and /nilai-ujian, menu items in the Ujian section, gateway prefixes /exam-grading and /exam-scores.
